# Evaluation goal

Evaluate NBA news for a fan who watches games later and does not want results spoiled.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Major.** Blockbuster trades, season-ending injuries to stars, major league decisions.
- **7-8: Important.** Notable trades and signings, significant injuries to starters, coaching changes, suspensions.
- **5-6: Notable.** Minor roster moves, short-term injuries, rumors from reliable insiders.
- **3-4: Low.** Speculation, routine roster moves.
- **0-2: Exclude.** Any game result, recap, highlights, box score, standings, playoff seeding outcome, or player statistics from a specific game. Score these 0 regardless of importance: the reader must not see results.
