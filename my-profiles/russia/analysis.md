# Evaluation goal

Evaluate how important a news item about Russia is for an ordinary reader living in Russia.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Major.** Events affecting most people in the country: big changes in prices, taxes, the ruble, key rate, internet or communications restrictions, nationwide disasters.
- **7-8: Important.** Notable economic news, new rules that change daily life, large incidents with wide resonance, major company news.
- **5-6: Notable.** Industry news, regional events with national resonance.
- **3-4: Low.** Routine regional news, minor incidents, local business.
- **0-2: Noise.** Crime chronicles, celebrity gossip, horoscopes, promotional material.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
