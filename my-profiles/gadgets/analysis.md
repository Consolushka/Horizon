# Evaluation goal

Evaluate news about gadgets and consumer software for a reader who uses an iPhone or Android phone and a Mac, and follows Apple and Google.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Major.** New flagship devices (iPhone, Pixel, Mac), major OS releases (iOS, Android, macOS), big platform policy changes.
- **7-8: Important.** Notable features in OS updates, credible leaks about upcoming flagships, significant app or service changes, serious security issues for users.
- **5-6: Notable.** Minor updates, beta builds, mid-range devices.
- **3-4: Low.** Rumors from weak sources, minor app updates.
- **0-2: Noise.** Deals, sales, accessories, how-to articles, sponsored content, opinion columns and editorials without new facts.

At equal significance prefer macOS, iPhone, and Pixel news.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
